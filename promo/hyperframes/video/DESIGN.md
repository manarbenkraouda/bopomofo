# Bopomofo promo: design

Source: promo/storyboard.md and frontend/src/components/mascot/Bobo.tsx.

## Style Prompt

Warm, friendly, and precise. Cream paper backgrounds, rounded app cards with soft shadows, and Bobo the Formosan black bear as the star on a light cream disc. The white V collar is the graphic motif: V wipes, V marks behind cards and on the end card. Motion is bouncy for Bobo (pop, squash and stretch, bob, hop) and crisp for UI (bars grow, keys press, chips pop).

## Colors

- Background: #f5f1e8 (cream). Cards: #fffdf8. Lines: #e3dbcc.
- Ink: #24201b. Muted: #7b7367 (large text only).
- Accent red: #b5452f. Accent soft: #f3dcd3.
- OK: #2f9466. Gold: #d39418. Bad: #d23f3f.
- Tones: 1 #2f9466, 2 #d39418, 3 #3b82c4, 4 #b5452f, neutral #7b7367.
- Bobo disc: #fffaf1 to #f1e6d6.

## Typography

- Latin: Geist (800 for headlines, 400-500 for body), Geist Mono for numbers, keys, and data.
- Hanzi and Zhuyin: Microsoft JhengHei (bold for display).
- Headlines 84-110 px, secondary 40-48 px, UI labels 28 px or more.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not put Bobo on a dark background, crop his chest, or cover his white V collar with text, UI, or confetti.
- Do not place text or key UI outside x 80-940, y 240-1420.
- Do not show provider, model, price, or debug text.
- No more than 7 words per headline; every text line stays at least 1.2 s.
- No dark full-screen gradients, no neon, no gradient text.
